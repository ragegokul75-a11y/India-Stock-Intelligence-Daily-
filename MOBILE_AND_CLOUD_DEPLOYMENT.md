# 📱 How to Use on Your Mobile Phone (While Traveling or Away From Laptop)

You have **2 easy ways** to use **India Stock Intelligence Daily** on your mobile phone:

---

## Option 1: 100% Free 24/7 Cloud Deployment (Works Even When Your Laptop is Turned OFF!)
This is the best option for **travel, office, and daily mobile use** because it runs 24/7 on the cloud—you never need to carry or turn on your laptop.

### Step-by-Step (Takes 3 Minutes, Free Forever):
1. **Create a Free GitHub Account (if you don't have one):**
   - Go to https://github.com and sign in.
   - Click **"+" (New Repository)** → Name it `india-stock-intelligence-daily` → Choose **Private** (or Public) → Click **Create repository**.
   - Upload the files from `D:\chatgpt\India Stock Intelligence Daily` (or run `Push_To_GitHub.bat`).
2. **Connect to Free Streamlit Cloud:**
   - Go to https://share.streamlit.io on your browser or phone and sign in with GitHub.
   - Click **"Create app"** → Select your repository `india-stock-intelligence-daily`.
   - Set **Main file path** to `app.py` and click **Deploy!**
3. **Save to Your Mobile Phone Home Screen Like an App:**
   - Open your new `https://....streamlit.app` link on your phone's Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
   - Tap the browser menu (**⋮** or **Share**) → Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
   - Now you have an app icon on your phone!
   - **Automatic Daily Cloud Updates:** Because we added [.github/workflows/daily_nse_intelligence.yml](file:///D:/chatgpt/India%20Stock%20Intelligence%20Daily/.github/workflows/daily_nse_intelligence.yml), GitHub's cloud automatically runs the NSE sync and Bull/Bear/Judge debate at **9:15 AM IST (Market Open)** and **6:15 PM IST (Market Close)** Monday–Friday even when your laptop is off!

---

## Option 2: Instant 1-Click Mobile Link From Your PC (`Start_Mobile_Link.bat`)
If your laptop is on at home and you are going out with your phone:
1. Double-click **[Start_Mobile_Link.bat](file:///D:/chatgpt/India%20Stock%20Intelligence%20Daily/Start_Mobile_Link.bat)** in `D:\chatgpt\India Stock Intelligence Daily`.
2. A window will pop up and show a public `https://....lhr.life` link (and a QR code right in the window!).
3. Scan the QR code with your phone camera (or open that `https://....lhr.life` link on WhatsApp/Chrome on your phone)—it works on 4G/5G anywhere!
